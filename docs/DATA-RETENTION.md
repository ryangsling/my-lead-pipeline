# Data retention and contact erasure

## Hub records

Set **Data retention (days)** in workspace Settings. The default is 90 days and admins can set 1–3650. The authenticated `/api/cron/sweep` applies the setting to active and archived workspaces. A day is 24 hours. The sweep measures call age from the end of the call, with the last activity, start or row creation as fallbacks. It ages contacts from their most recent identified activity or creation, and keeps a contact linked to a still-recent conversation, booking or job. For a booking, the sweep uses its creation time or scheduled start, whichever is earlier. Existing bookings whose creation date is unknown use the earlier of their scheduled start and the migration date. Standalone inbox, outbox, intake and tool records use their stored creation or receipt time. Historical receipts without an attributable workspace use the shortest configured workspace interval or 90 days when no workspace exists.

Open a contact and choose **Delete contact** to erase it without waiting for expiry. The hub erases linked contact details, CRM notes and tags, conversations, transcripts, summaries, structured output, playback links, raw Vapi inbox receipts, urgent events, tool inputs and receipts, jobs, and local notification logs and pending sends. It also checks known phone/email fields in pending Vapi and public booking payloads that have no CRM link yet. It clears appointment notes, links to the contact and conversations, and private data in any unresolved calendar operation. The appointment and its held interval remain. A fresh enquiry can create a new contact. Known Vapi call IDs remain blocked from webhook, tool and transfer replay using a workspace-scoped keyed hash. Review any relevant historical records without verified linkage separately, including personal information in unrelated free text.

Settings shows **Data deletion history**. **Hub records** verifies the local transaction. Vapi and Google rows share its reference and show pending work, retries or verification. The authenticated `GET /api/contacts/deletions?workspaceId=<id>&requestId=<reference>` also exposes status, attempts and timestamps. Save the reference in the restricted request tracker and check all provider entries before reporting completion. The operational history and call hashes remain for as long as the workspace exists. Pending or manually reviewed provider work keeps an encrypted locator with no caller name, transcript, phone, email or signed URL. Successful verification removes the locator. Keep the Hub encryption key and associated deletion history when moving or recovering the database, since the key also protects the replay hashes.

## Vapi and Google

- **Vapi.** The sweep verifies that the provider call belongs to the workspace’s authenticated assistant or an unambiguous historical workspace mapping before using `DELETE /call/{id}`. It checks that the call and all five authenticated recording lookup endpoints return 404 or 410. A lost response remains retryable and ownership is checkpointed before deletion. **Needs review** means the hub could not verify the resource or account. Confirm ownership and retention with the provider before taking further action. For expired credentials or temporary API failures, reconnect the original Vapi account and let the next scheduled retry run. The sweep works from authenticated call IDs captured by the hub. Include provider-only calls that never reached the hub, custom storage and media exports in the provider account’s own retention procedure. Confirm revocation of a previously issued signed URL and underlying provider media retention on a disposable account before promising either.
- **Google Calendar.** The sweep changes the title of a verified hub event to “Appointment” and clears its description. Those are the two fields the hub populates with client details. It keeps the existing event, time, transparency, status and user-managed fields such as location, attendees, reminders, attachments and additional metadata. The user should keep contact details out of those fields and, when they were added independently, remove or retain them under the calendar owner’s policy. Calendar time/service/capacity metadata has no automatic deletion schedule. On old unmarked events, the hub requires the stored calendar and event IDs, a unique local mapping, and an interval matching the erasure snapshot or the hub’s current or pending schedule. Unmapped or ambiguous events stay visible for review. Google trash, calendar exports, attendee copies, revision history and provider backups follow Google or the calendar owner’s terms.

## Delivered copies, backups and restores

| Copy | Responsibility and timing |
| --- | --- |
| Telegram and email | The hub purges its local outbox and retry state. A send already submitted to a transport can still be accepted. Delivered Telegram messages, email, forwards, exports, recipients’ backups and the providers’ delivery logs remain under the workspace owner, recipients and provider policies. The hub has no sender-side recall. Workspace owners should configure their owned destination history to expire within their selected workspace retention window where the channel supports it. For a contact erasure, ask authorized recipients to remove matching delivered copies and record any provider or recipient limits before closing the request. |
| Managed Postgres snapshots and point-in-time recovery | The hub purges the live primary database. Managed Supabase backups and PITR can still contain the old records until their configured windows expire. Before using real data, check the actual snapshot and PITR intervals in Supabase, record the intervals in the operator inventory, and disclose the longer interval to the workspace owner. |
| Manual database dumps and other operator exports | Keep cutover dumps access-restricted and encrypted and destroy them when the cutover is verified, within seven days of creation. Record any separately approved export’s owner and destruction date in the operator inventory. A workspace’s retention setting does not reach those files. |
| Restoration | Restore in an isolated environment with the outbound jobs and webhook traffic paused. Preserve post-backup deletion history and restricted request references outside the restored copy. Restore or reconcile that history, reapply contact requests made after the backup, and run retention before restoring normal access or outbound jobs. Verify that deleted call IDs still reject replay and that provider work did not lose its checkpoints. |

Before enabling production retention, exercise a synthetic contact and call on a disposable Vapi account, keep a pre-deletion signed playback URL, verify the call’s disappearance in the Vapi console and recording routes and verify the old URL. Exercise Google cleanup and a reschedule on a disposable calendar, and confirm the real backup inventory and recipient handling terms. The test suite covers Postgres state and stateful provider mocks.
